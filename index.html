01 — About

Built on data. Focused on what it tells you.

I'm a Senior Data & Observability Consultant with 7+ years architecting enterprise monitoring platforms, data pipelines, and analytics solutions for complex IT environments. At TekStream Solutions I design scalable data ingestion architectures, distributed systems observability, and IT service intelligence frameworks for enterprise clients — with deep platform expertise built primarily on Splunk Cloud, Enterprise, and ITSI.

Most of my work starts in the same place: a client has more operational data than they can use, and no clear line from that data to service health. I define the monitoring strategy, model the data, build the ingestion pipelines, and stand up the service-health frameworks that make it legible — across spans, traces, logs, and metrics, spanning Kubernetes clusters, Linux, and Windows infrastructure.

The part of that work I want more of is the advisory side. My best engagements are the ones where I become the customer's go-to expert — in the planning sessions, mapping where their observability practice should be a year out, and making the case for capabilities they haven't adopted yet. That's the direction I'm building toward — customer-facing technical leadership, wherever the job is shaping what gets built and why rather than only building it, and helping a customer see what the platform could do for them before they've asked for it.

I already do a version of this today — developing observability roadmaps with client stakeholders, serving as the platform subject matter expert my team escalates to, and mentoring junior consultants. Alongside the technical certifications, I hold Splunk and Grafana sales accreditations, because being useful in the room where the decision gets made means speaking to business value, not just architecture.

I'm also genuinely interested in where this field is heading. I run a self-hosted home AI server on Llama, test against Grok and Claude, and recently built a prototype AI observability platform to work through how monitoring LLM applications differs from monitoring traditional services. That curiosity is exactly what I'd bring to a customer conversation about what's next.

Featured project 2026

AI Observability Platform

A prototype observability system for LLM applications, built to explore how monitoring AI systems differs from monitoring traditional services — drawing on ideas from Galileo, Arize, Braintrust, Langfuse, and Fiddler.

  • Trace management — captures LLM interactions, tool use, and agent operations using OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions.
  • Test reproduction — production traces become test cases you can re-run against a different prompt or model.
  • Evaluation — LLM-based scoring for faithfulness, relevance, and hallucination detection, with judge version tracking.
  • Versioned artifacts — prompts and datasets stored as immutable versions.
  • Safety screening — real-time guardrails evaluate outputs before delivery.
  • Cost attribution — per-run provider key tracking to correlate spend against output quality.
